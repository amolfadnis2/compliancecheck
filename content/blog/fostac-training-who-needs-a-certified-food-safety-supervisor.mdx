FSSAI expects most licensed food businesses to have at least one trained **Food Safety Supervisor** certified through **FoSTaC**, and inspectors check for this certification as part of routine and risk-based inspections.

## Key facts at a glance

- **FoSTaC** stands for **Food Safety Training and Certification**, run by FSSAI.
- Most licensed food businesses - manufacturers, caterers, restaurants, larger retail outlets - need at least one certified **Food Safety Supervisor**.
- Certification is delivered through **FSSAI-empanelled training partners**.
- FoSTaC certificates have a **fixed validity period**, after which **refresher training and recertification** is required.
- Food Safety Supervisor certification is one of the records typically checked during **FSSAI inspections**.
- Training levels and requirements can vary by **business category and scale**.
- Registration and enrolment are done through the **official FoSTaC portal**.

## What FoSTaC Actually Is

FoSTaC is FSSAI's structured training and certification framework aimed at building food safety competence within food businesses, rather than relying solely on inspections after the fact. It trains people who directly handle food safety responsibilities - most notably the **Food Safety Supervisor** role that many businesses are required to designate.

Training covers practical food safety knowledge: hygienic handling, storage, temperature control, cross-contamination prevention, cleaning protocols, and understanding of applicable FSSAI standards for that business category.

## Who Needs a Certified Food Safety Supervisor

| Business type | Typical requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Restaurants and food service outlets | Certified Food Safety Supervisor generally required |
| Food manufacturers and processors | Certified Food Safety Supervisor generally required, often with category-specific modules |
| Caterers | Certified Food Safety Supervisor generally required |
| Retail food outlets (larger scale) | Certified Food Safety Supervisor generally required |
| Very small/petty food businesses under Registration | Requirements may be lighter; confirm current applicability for your specific category |

Exact applicability depends on your FSSAI licence category and business type, so it is worth confirming directly against current FSSAI guidance for your specific business rather than assuming a blanket rule.

## Why It Matters Beyond Just Compliance

A trained Food Safety Supervisor is the person on the ground who is expected to actually apply food safety practices day to day - handling temperature logs, spotting contamination risks, and managing staff hygiene practices. Beyond satisfying an FSSAI requirement, this reduces the practical risk of the kind of incident that triggers a complaint-based inspection or reputational damage.

## How Certification Works

1. **Identify the applicable FoSTaC training level** for your business category (basic, advanced, or trainer-level, depending on role and business type).
2. **Enrol through an FSSAI-empanelled training partner** via the official FoSTaC portal.
3. **Complete the training**, which can include classroom sessions, practical demonstrations, and assessments depending on the level.
4. **Receive certification**, valid for a fixed period.
5. **Track the expiry date** and plan for refresher training before it lapses.

## Keeping Certification Current

Because FoSTaC certificates expire and need renewal, businesses with high staff turnover or multiple outlets should track certification status actively rather than assuming it is a one-time task. A lapsed certificate found during an inspection creates the same kind of gap as never having certified staff in the first place.

## Practical Steps for a Food Business

- Maintain a **register of FoSTaC-certified staff** with certification and expiry dates.
- Ensure **each outlet or unit** has coverage, especially for multi-location businesses.
- **Renew ahead of expiry** rather than waiting for an inspection to expose a lapse.
- Keep certificates **physically or digitally accessible** for inspection purposes.
