How much will your PF actually be worth when you retire? Most PF calculators answer that with one fixed salary and one fixed interest rate, which is why their numbers rarely match real life. Your salary goes up every year, the wage ceiling just changed, and a big chunk of your employer's contribution never reaches your PF balance at all.

Our free **[PF calculator with salary hike](/calculator/pf)** models all of that, month by month, until the day you retire. This guide explains how PF is calculated in 2026, what drives your final EPF corpus, and how to use the calculator to plan around it.

## Key facts at a glance

- **EPF interest rate for FY 2025-26: 8.25%**, notified by EPFO on 1 July 2026.
- **PF wage ceiling: Rs 25,000 a month** from 17 September 2026 (up from Rs 15,000).
- **Maximum mandatory employee PF: Rs 3,000 a month** (12% of Rs 25,000), up from Rs 1,800.
- **Employee contribution:** 12% of Basic + DA. **Employer contribution:** 12%, of which **8.33% goes to EPS** (pension).
- **EDLI life cover:** between Rs 2.5 lakh and Rs 7 lakh, paid by your employer.
- **Tax-free interest limit:** own contributions up to Rs 2.5 lakh a year.

## How PF is calculated: the basic formula

Every month, two contributions go in:

| Contribution | Rate | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employee | 12% of Basic + DA | Your EPF account |
| Employer | 3.67% of Basic + DA (more if your wage is above the EPS ceiling) | Your EPF account |
| Employer | 8.33% of Basic + DA, up to the EPS ceiling | Employees' Pension Scheme (EPS) |
| Employer | 0.5% EDLI + 0.5% admin charges | Insurance and EPFO admin, not your account |

So on a Basic + DA of Rs 40,000 with PF on full Basic, you put in Rs 4,800 a month and your employer puts in Rs 4,800. But only Rs 2,717 of the employer's share lands in your EPF balance. The other Rs 2,083 buys your EPS pension. This is the single most common reason people think "my employer isn't paying the full 12%". They are; it is just split.

Interest is worked out monthly on your running balance but credited only once a year, on 31 March. That is why your effective return comes out slightly under the declared rate. Our calculator follows the same method EPFO uses. For the full rulebook, see [EPF Scheme 2026: what actually changed for employers](/blog/epf-scheme-2026-what-actually-changed-for-employers).

## The 2026 wage ceiling change and what it means for you

On 17 September 2026 the EPF wage ceiling went up from Rs 15,000 to Rs 25,000 a month. Two things follow:

1. **If your Basic + DA is Rs 25,000 or less**, PF is now mandatory on your full wage.
2. **If your employer caps PF at the ceiling**, your contribution jumps from Rs 1,800 to Rs 3,000 a month, but then stays frozen there however much your salary rises.

Whether the EPS ceiling also moved to Rs 25,000 is still under clarification (one reading of G.S.R. 890(E) keeps it at Rs 15,000). The calculator lets you pick either reading under **More options**, so you can see how your pension changes under both.

## Full Basic vs capped wage: the choice that matters most

Ask HR one question: **"Do you pay PF on my full Basic, or on the capped wage?"** The answer changes your retirement corpus more than any other input.

Here is the same 28-year-old with a Basic + DA of Rs 40,000, an 8% yearly hike, a Rs 2 lakh opening balance, retiring at 58, at 8.25%:

| PF basis | Corpus at 58 | In today's money (6% inflation) | Total you put in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full Basic | **Rs 3.53 crore** | Rs 61 lakh | Rs 70.3 lakh |
| Capped at Rs 25,000 | **Rs 79.5 lakh** | Rs 13.8 lakh | Rs 12.8 lakh |

That is a difference of more than Rs 2.7 crore at retirement. On the capped basis, your salary hikes simply don't reach your PF. You can [see the capped scenario in the calculator](/calculator/pf?bs=capped) and switch back to Full Basic to compare.

If your employer caps PF and you want a bigger corpus, the usual fix is **VPF** (below). If you are on the employer side and redesigning salary structures, read [how the Code on Wages 50% rule rewrites your salary structure](/blog/the-50-wages-rule-how-the-code-on-wages-rewrites-your-salary-structure), because low-Basic structures now pull allowances back into the PF wage.

## VPF: should you top up your PF?

Voluntary Provident Fund (VPF) lets you contribute more than 12% of your Basic. It earns the same rate as EPF, with sovereign backing, but with no employer match.

Adding 5% of Basic as VPF in our example lifts the corpus from Rs 3.53 crore to **Rs 4.28 crore**. The catch is tax: once your own PF + VPF contributions cross Rs 2.5 lakh a year, interest on the excess is taxable every year. The calculator flags the year you cross that line and estimates the tax. [Try 5% VPF](/calculator/pf?v=5) and move the slider to find your sweet spot.

## Your EPS pension and EDLI life cover

Two benefits most people forget they have:

**EPS pension.** Pension = pensionable salary x years of service / 70, with pensionable salary capped at the wage ceiling. You need 10 years of service, it starts at 58, and the minimum is Rs 1,000 a month. Serving 20 years or more to 58 adds two bonus years. In our example, the calculator estimates an EPS pension of about **Rs 12,300 a month** (on the Rs 25,000 EPS ceiling reading).

**EDLI life cover.** If you die in service, EPFO pays your nominee 35 times your average monthly wage plus half your average PF balance (that part capped at Rs 1.75 lakh), between Rs 2.5 lakh and Rs 7 lakh. No premium comes out of your salary. Before you buy term insurance, read [EDLI vs term life: what your PF already covers](/blog/edli-vs-term-life-what-your-pf-already-covers).

## Is your PF interest taxable?

- **Old tax regime:** your PF and VPF contributions count towards the Rs 1.5 lakh Section 80C limit. The calculator adds that saving to your return when you choose the old regime.
- **New tax regime:** no 80C deduction.
- **Both regimes:** interest on your own contributions above Rs 2.5 lakh a year (Rs 5 lakh if your employer doesn't contribute) is taxable each year.
- **Employer contributions** to PF, NPS and superannuation above Rs 7.5 lakh a year are taxed as a perquisite.

The calculator shows a post-tax corpus alongside the headline number, so you are not planning on money that will go to the taxman.
