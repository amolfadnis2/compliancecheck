**ComplianceCheck now lets Chartered Accountants, Company Secretaries, lawyers and compliance consultants issue compliance gap reports to their clients under their own firm's name.** Tick one box on the first screen of any paid assessment, add your firm details and logo, and the detailed PDF your client receives carries your branding on the cover, in a "Prepared By" box, in the footer and in the closing contact section. The price is the same as a normal assessment, and you set your own fee.

Three sample branded reports are available to download further down this page.

## Key facts at a glance

- Works on all **seven paid assessments**: Statutory Health, Labour Code, DPDP, POSH, State-wise Compliance, Food Business (FSSAI) and Auto Dealer.
- **Self-serve.** No sign-up, partner application or approval. One checkbox on the intake form turns it on.
- **Your brand on the report:** firm name, logo, brand colour, designation, phone, email and website on the cover and in a "Prepared By" box.
- **Footer on every page:** "Prepared by Your Firm | Powered by ComplianceCheck.co.in".
- **File name** is `Firm-Client-Assessment`, so the PDF you forward already carries your name.
- **Same price** as a direct buyer. You pay at checkout and bill your client separately. ComplianceCheck issues the GST invoice to you.
- **Client contact details are not collected.** Only the client's company name, state, headcount and industry are asked for.
- **Delivery in your name:** email sent as "Your Firm via ComplianceCheck" with replies to you, or a WhatsApp link worded from your firm.

## How partner mode works, step by step

1. **Pick the assessment** your client needs, for example the [Statutory Health Check](/assessment/statutory-health) or the [DPDP Gap Assessment](/assessment/dpdp).
2. **Tick "I'm a CA / CS / lawyer / consultant filling this in for a client"** on the company details step.
3. **Add your firm details:** firm name, your designation (CA, CS, CMA, Advocate, Consultant or Other), city and website. Upload a logo (PNG or JPEG, up to 300 KB) and pick a brand colour.
4. **Use your own name, email and phone.** In partner mode these fields are yours. The company name, state, employee count and industry fields describe the client.
5. **Confirm the details are your own** using the required attestation checkbox.
6. **Answer the assessment** with your client's information. The questions, scoring and legal rules are the same as for a direct buyer.
7. **Review the branded summary page.** It shows your firm in a header bar along with the score, critical gaps and top actions.
8. **Pay at checkout.** Your email is already filled in. ComplianceCheck is the merchant of record and issues the GST invoice to you.
9. **Deliver the report:** download the PDF, email it from the report page, or share a signed WhatsApp link that stays valid for 30 days.

The next time you open an assessment on the same browser, your firm details are filled in for you, so a second or third client takes only a few minutes.

## Why a white-label report helps your practice

**It turns a question into an engagement.** When a client asks "do we need to worry about DPDP?", a scored gap report with a dated action plan is a more useful answer than an email, and it leads naturally to policy drafting, registrations or a retainer.

**It is priced for resale.** You pay the standard per-assessment price with no subscription or minimum volume, and set your own professional fee on top. For how this compares with retainers and software subscriptions, see [compliance software: what to actually look for](/blog/compliance-software-what-to-actually-look-for).

**It is consistent.** Every client in the same segment is assessed against the same rules, with the same legal references and peer comparison. That makes the report useful as a baseline before a [compliance due diligence](/blog/compliance-due-diligence-what-investors-actually-check), and as a before-and-after record when you reassess.

**It keeps you as the client's point of contact.** The report email comes from "Your Firm via ComplianceCheck", replies come to you, and the report closes with your contact details.

**It keeps client data to a minimum.** You do not enter your client's email or phone number, and your client's company name and contact details are never sent to our analytics.

## What partner mode does not do

To be clear about the limits:

- **It does not verify your credentials.** Firm details are self-declared and the report says so. Only enter your own name, firm and designation.
- **It is not legal advice.** The report is a structured gap assessment. Your professional judgment, and any advice you give the client, remains yours.
- **There is no volume discount yet.** Every report is charged at the standard price.
- **Your client cannot yet fill it in through a link from you.** For now, you complete the assessment on the client's behalf.
- **The free calculators are not branded.** The gratuity, CTC, PF and penalty exposure calculators stay as they are.
