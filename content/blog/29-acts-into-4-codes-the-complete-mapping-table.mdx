India's 4 Labour Codes consolidate **29 separate central labour laws** into a single, more consistent framework covering wages, industrial relations, social security, and workplace safety.

## Key facts at a glance

- The 4 Labour Codes consolidate **29 central labour laws** that were previously administered separately.
- **Code on Wages, 2019** merges **4 laws**, including the Minimum Wages Act and Payment of Bonus Act.
- **Industrial Relations Code, 2020** merges **3 laws**, including the Industrial Disputes Act and Trade Unions Act.
- **Code on Social Security, 2020** merges **9 laws**, including the EPF Act, ESI Act, Maternity Benefit Act, and Gratuity Act.
- **Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020** merges **13 laws**, including the Factories Act and Contract Labour Act.
- All four Codes came into force nationally on **21 November 2025**, though state-level rule notification is still in progress through 2026.

## Why 29 laws became 4

Before the Codes, India's labour law framework had grown into a patchwork of Acts passed across nearly a century, each with its own definitions, registers, forms, and enforcement machinery. An employer with a moderately complex workforce could be simultaneously subject to a dozen or more separate Acts, each demanding its own compliance process. The consolidation into 4 Codes was designed to simplify this into one coherent framework per subject area - wages, industrial relations, social security, and occupational safety - with consistent definitions used throughout.

## What does not change

The consolidation is primarily structural - it brings existing protections and obligations under one roof and standardizes definitions like "wages" and "employee." It does not, by itself, remove the underlying protections these 29 Acts were built to provide; things like minimum wage payment, industrial dispute resolution, safety standards, and social security contributions remain core obligations, just organized differently.

## What changes in practice

The biggest practical shifts are a single wages definition applied consistently (with the 50% allowance cap), simplified registration and licensing processes designed to replace multiple overlapping registrations, expanded social security coverage for gig and platform workers, and consolidated inspection and dispute-resolution mechanisms. Employers that previously managed compliance Act-by-Act will need to shift to thinking Code-by-Code, which also changes which government department or portal they interact with for a given process.
