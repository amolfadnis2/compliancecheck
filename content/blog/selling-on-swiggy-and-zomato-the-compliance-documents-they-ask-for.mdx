Swiggy and Zomato both require a specific set of compliance documents before they will onboard a restaurant, cloud kitchen, or home-based food business, and the FSSAI licence is the non-negotiable core of that list. Getting these documents ready before you apply speeds up onboarding significantly.

## Key facts at a glance

- A valid **FSSAI licence or registration number** is mandatory for every outlet listed on Swiggy or Zomato.
- **GST registration** is typically required once turnover crosses the statutory threshold, and platforms usually collect a GSTIN during onboarding regardless.
- **Shop and establishment registration** is commonly requested as proof of legal business existence.
- **Bank account and PAN details** are needed for payout settlement and tax reporting.
- Licence numbers displayed on listings can be **cross-checked by customers and regulators**, so accuracy matters.
- FSSAI licences issued from **1 April 2026 no longer carry a fixed expiry date**, running until suspended, cancelled, or the business closes.

## The core document checklist

### FSSAI licence or registration

This is the single most important document. Depending on your annual turnover, you will need either a Basic Registration (small businesses, turnover up to the registration threshold), a State Licence (mid-sized, up to the state licence threshold), or a Central Licence (large, multi-state, or specific categories). As of the 2026 amendment regulations, the registration turnover threshold is **Rs 1.5 crore** and the state licence threshold is **Rs 50 crore**.

### GST registration

Platforms need your GSTIN to correctly apply tax on orders and remit dues. Even sellers below the mandatory GST threshold are often asked to register, because aggregators handle tax collection at source on behalf of many restaurant partners.

### Shop and establishment registration

This state-issued registration confirms your outlet is a legitimate commercial establishment. Requirements and processes vary by state, so check your specific state labour department's portal.

### PAN and bank details

Needed for KYC, payout processing, and TDS compliance on platform commission and settlements.

### FSSAI-linked signage and menu labelling

Platforms may also expect your menu to reflect any mandatory food safety disclosures (allergens, non-veg/veg marking) consistent with FSSAI labelling norms.

## Common onboarding delays and how to avoid them

Most onboarding delays come from mismatched details - a business name on the FSSAI licence that does not match the GST certificate, or an expired licence that was not renewed in time. Keep all documents under the same registered business name and check expiry dates before applying, especially if your licence predates the 1 April 2026 no-expiry change and is still on the older fixed-term system.

Platforms also periodically re-verify documents, particularly around FSSAI licence renewal cycles or after a compliance complaint, so treat onboarding documentation as something to keep current, not a one-time submission.
