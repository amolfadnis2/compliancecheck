**The Ministry of MSME has drafted a framework to extend ZED (Zero Defect Zero Effect) certification to services MSMEs** - starting with transport, accommodation, food and beverage, travel agencies, tour operators and hospitals, measured on about 20 service-quality parameters. **As of 9 November 2026, no guidelines, fees, subsidy rates or launch date have been notified.** Don't wait for them. What the parameters will reward - delivery records, written processes, complaint logs, vendor contracts, facility upkeep - costs almost nothing to build and pays off with or without a certificate.

## Summary: the 60-second version

- **What's new:** The Economic Times (9 Nov 2026) reports a drafted framework with ~20 service parameters, plus 4-5 extra parameters for exporters. The source is unnamed officials; nothing is notified.
- **What's confirmed:** ZED's 2022 guidelines always planned a "Phase 2" for services. Manufacturing ZED runs today with Bronze/Silver/Gold levels, fees of Rs 8,000 / 32,000 / 72,000 and 80/60/50% subsidies.
- **What's unknown:** levels, fees, subsidies, assessment method and start date for services.
- **What to do now:** update your [Udyam registration](/blog/udyam-registration-what-it-actually-gets-you), fix licence gaps, and start keeping service-level, complaint, maintenance and vendor records.
- **What not to do:** pay anyone to "register you for ZED services". It doesn't exist yet, and portal registration is free.

## What was announced - and how solid it is

| Item | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Services planned as ZED "Phase 2" (with "ZED Exports") | **Confirmed** - ZED Guidelines 2022, ZED portal, QCI FAQ |
| Framework with ~20 service parameters drafted | Reported, not notified |
| First sectors: transport, accommodation, F&B, travel agents, tour operators, hospitals | Reported |
| 4-5 exporter parameters (export documentation, traceability, origin verification) | Reported |
| Bronze/Silver/Gold levels for services | Not announced; likely by analogy |
| Fees, subsidy rates, launch date | Not announced |

The direction is solid; the detail is not. QCI and the National Productivity Council have been developing service-sector parameters since at least August 2026. But the ministry's last formal word - a Lok Sabha reply on 12 March 2026 - said ZED "primarily covers manufacturing MSMEs". **Treat the parameter and sector lists as a well-sourced draft, not a rulebook.**

Why it matters: services make up about **38% of India's MSME base** (against 20% manufacturing and 42% trading), and until now none of them could access ZED-linked subsidies, bank concessions or state incentives.

## How ZED works for manufacturers today

Services ZED will almost certainly be built on the existing scheme, so this is the best preview available.

- **Run by** the Office of the DC (MSME) with the Quality Council of India (QCI). Launched 28 April 2022 under the MSME Champions Scheme.
- **Eligibility:** any Udyam-registered MSME. Registration on **zed.msme.gov.in** is free.
- **ZED Pledge** earns a **Rs 10,000 joining reward** (valid one year).
- **Assessment:** document review plus an on-site visit for Bronze (since September 2024); remote for Silver; on-site for Gold, by QCI-accredited agencies.
- **Statutory undertaking:** you declare you meet all applicable laws. Certificates can be withdrawn if you don't.
- **Validity:** 3 years, with surveillance visits.
- **Exemptions:** valid ISO 9001, 14001, 45001 and 50001 certificates exempt matching parameters - a useful precedent for NABH, FSSAI or ISO holders in services.

| Level | Fee | Micro pays (80% subsidy) | Small (60%) | Medium (50%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bronze | Rs 8,000 | Rs 0 after joining reward | Rs 0 | Rs 0 |
| Silver | Rs 32,000 | Rs 6,400 | Rs 12,800 | Rs 16,000 |
| Gold | Rs 72,000 | Rs 14,400 | Rs 28,800 | Rs 36,000 |

Add **+10%** for women/SC/ST-owned units or those in North-East, Himalayan, LWE, island or aspirational districts, **+5%** for SFURTI/MSE-CDP clusters, and **100% subsidy for women-owned MSMEs** (since November 2023).

**Benefits after certification:** up to Rs 50,000 towards testing and other certifications, handholding up to Rs 2 lakh, technology upgradation up to Rs 3 lakh, processing-fee and rate concessions at **19 financial institutions**, **85% CGTMSE guarantee cover**, and incentives in **22 states/UTs**. There is currently **no** procurement preference for ZED-certified firms - don't expect it to win tenders on its own.

**The reality check:** the ZED dashboard (5 November 2026) shows 10.86 lakh registrations, 7.72 lakh Bronze, but only about 11,000 Silver and 6,300 Gold certificates. Bronze is near-free and easy; Silver and Gold are where the process work - and the bigger bank and state benefits - sit. Expect services ZED to follow the same pattern.

## The service parameters, decoded

The ministry has not published definitions. These are **our reading**, based on the matching manufacturing parameters and standard service-quality practice. Use them as a preparation guide.

| Parameter (reported) | What it likely tests | Start now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Timely service delivery | Did you deliver what you promised, when you promised? (Manufacturing measures "On Time, In Full".) | Define 3-5 service promises; log them weekly |
| Service quality management | Quality policy, standards, error correction | Written quality policy; error register with root causes |
| Facility management | Premises, vehicles, kitchens, equipment - cleanliness, safety, maintenance | Maintenance calendar; fire-safety, pest-control, housekeeping logs |
| Process control | Standardised, repeatable processes | SOPs for your 5-10 core processes, with checklists and sign-offs |
| Customer management | Feedback, complaints, customer data | Complaint register with closure times; privacy notice |
| Partners, vendors, franchisees | Control over third parties | Written agreements with service standards; periodic reviews |
| Outsourced operations | You remain responsible for outsourced work | List of outsourced activities, contract and SLA for each, labour-compliance evidence |

The Economic Times named only seven. **It is our inference, not reported fact,** that the remaining ~13 will adapt manufacturing parameters such as leadership, workplace safety, HR and training, energy and environment, risk management and technology adoption.

Two of these overlap directly with laws you already have to follow. Customer data handling sits under the [DPDP Act](/blog/dpdp-act-2023-readiness-checklist), and outsourced housekeeping, security or driving staff bring [contract labour obligations](/blog/contract-labour-when-the-principal-employer-pays-the-pf) to the principal employer. An assessor will look at both.

## What it means for your sector

**Hospitals and clinics.** The biggest overlap. NABH's Entry-Level Certification for small healthcare organisations (up to 50 beds) and SHCO accreditation already test much of the same ground, and NABH, like ZED, sits under QCI. If you hold NABH, you will likely meet much of services ZED. If you don't, get NABH first - it is linked to insurer empanelment, so its commercial value is proven. See our [hospitals and clinics compliance checklist](/blog/the-complete-compliance-checklist-for-hospitals-and-clinics-in-india) and [DPDP guide for clinics](/blog/dpdp-for-clinics-hospitals-and-health-apps).

**Hotels, homestays and accommodation.** Ministry of Tourism star classification (HRACC) is mostly about **facilities**; ZED's reported parameters are about **process and delivery**. They complement each other. Small hotels relying on OTAs and outsourced housekeeping or laundry should watch the partner and outsourcing parameters. Start with our [hotels and hospitality checklist](/blog/the-complete-compliance-checklist-for-hotels-and-hospitality-in-india).

**Restaurants, cloud kitchens and caterers.** FSSAI registration or licence comes first. FSSAI's [Hygiene Rating Scheme](/blog/hygiene-rating-and-third-party-fssai-audits) already checks much of what facility management and process control would cover. A good rating, a [FoSTaC-trained supervisor](/blog/fostac-training-who-needs-a-certified-food-safety-supervisor) and written kitchen SOPs will put you ahead. Franchise-heavy and [cloud kitchen](/blog/cloud-kitchen-compliance-from-day-one) brands should study the franchisee and aggregator-partner parameter.

**Travel agencies and tour operators.** Ministry of Tourism recognition and state tourism registrations are today's credibility markers. Services ZED would add measured **timely delivery** (confirmations, refunds, pickups) and **partner management** (hotels, transporters, guides). Whether the exporter parameters fit inbound operators selling to foreign agents is an open question - they sound product-focused.

**Transport and logistics.** Fleet operators already keep permits, fitness certificates, PUC and driver records. Vehicle maintenance logs map to facility management, trip punctuality data to timely delivery, and attached-vehicle arrangements to outsourced operations. Your GPS and trip data is ready-made evidence. See the [logistics and warehousing checklist](/blog/the-complete-compliance-checklist-for-logistics-and-warehousing-in-india).

## Exporters: the add-on module

The reported exporter add-on covers **export documentation, traceability and origin verification**. DGFT now issues Certificates of Origin only through its electronic platform (trade.gov.in), with an API since September 2026, and rules-of-origin proof matters more with each new FTA. Start a clean digital file per shipment: invoice, packing list, CoO, shipping bill and input-sourcing records with matching invoice numbers. Food exporters should also check the [import and export food licences](/blog/importing-or-exporting-food-licences-and-clearances) they need. ZED does not replace a destination market's own certification requirements.

## State incentives: check before you count on them

Twenty-two states/UTs have built ZED into their industrial policies. Examples: **Maharashtra** reimburses 50% of ZED fees up to Rs 50,000 (2025 policy, which covers services in its title); **Odisha** adds an 80% top-up subsidy; **Uttar Pradesh** reimburses up to 75% (max Rs 5 lakh) for quality certifications. But **many state MSME policies give fiscal incentives only to manufacturing units** - Odisha's explicitly excludes most service enterprises. Ask your District Industries Centre before you rely on state money.

## Readiness checklist for service SMEs

**This week**
- Udyam registration current, with the correct services NIC code and up-to-date turnover
- All licences valid: FSSAI, trade licence, shop and establishment, [fire NOC](/blog/fire-noc-in-india-who-needs-one-and-how-to-get-it), clinical establishment registration, transport permits, tourism registrations, GST
- Labour compliance documented: [PF/ESI](/blog/pf-esi-applicability-employer-guide), contractor licences, minimum wages

**Next 30 days**
- 3-5 written service promises (e.g. confirmation within 2 hours, OPD wait under 30 minutes) and a weekly log against them
- Quality policy and SOPs for 5-10 core processes
- Error/incident register and complaint register with resolution times
- Feedback collection and a privacy notice

**Next 60 days**
- Preventive maintenance calendar; housekeeping, pest-control and hygiene checklists; fire drill and training records
- List of every vendor, franchisee, aggregator and outsourced activity, each with a written SLA and review record
- Exporters: active IEC, DGFT digital CoO workflow, per-shipment document file

**Leverage what you have:** put ISO 9001, NABH, FSSAI hygiene rating and star-classification certificates in one folder. They are your strongest evidence and could become grounds for exemption (unconfirmed).

## Pitfalls to avoid

1. **Paying agents for "ZED services registration."** It doesn't exist yet; the portal is free.
2. **Chasing paper compliance.** Even Bronze now includes an on-site visit. Assessors check that SOPs are actually followed.
3. **Wrong Udyam data.** A wrong NIC code or stale turnover can block eligibility or put you in the wrong subsidy band.
4. **Ignoring the statutory undertaking.** ZED requires you to declare full legal compliance; certificates can be withdrawn. Fix licence gaps first - the [cost of non-compliance](/blog/the-real-cost-of-non-compliance-in-india) is far higher than the certificate's value.
5. **Assuming manufacturing benefits carry over.** State policies and bank schemes were written for manufacturing ZED.
6. **Skipping sector accreditation for ZED.** For hospitals (NABH) and food businesses (FSSAI), the sector credential has clearer commercial value today.

## Our take

This expansion is overdue. Services are the largest productive segment of India's MSME base and have been shut out of ZED's subsidies and concessions. But manufacturing ZED shows the pattern: easy Bronze certificates scale fast (7.7 lakh), while real upgrading at Silver and Gold stays rare (about 17,000 combined). The service businesses that gain most will be those that spend the next few months building real delivery data and documented processes - not the ones that rush for a badge. **Prepare now; certify when the guidelines are notified.**

The statutory undertaking is the part most businesses underestimate. A ZED assessor expects your licences, labour filings and registers to already be in order. ComplianceCheck's Statutory Health Check shows those gaps in a few minutes - worth fixing whether or not you go for certification.
