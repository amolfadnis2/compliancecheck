# India's Four Labor Codes: SME Compliance Thresholds and Triggers

All four Indian Labor Codes were officially notified for implementation on **November 21, 2025**, consolidating 29 central labor laws into a unified framework. For SMEs with 10-500 employees, this represents a fundamental restructuring of compliance obligations with new thresholds, definitions, and triggers that differ substantially from previous legislation.

## Critical threshold changes favor SMEs

The new codes raise several key thresholds that reduce compliance burdens for smaller establishments. Standing orders now apply at **300 workers** (up from 100), factory definitions require **20 workers with power** (up from 10), and prior government permission for retrenchment kicks in at **300 workers** (up from 100). However, some thresholds decreased—canteen requirements now trigger at **100 workers** (down from 250), and annual leave eligibility requires only **180 working days** (down from 240).

## Code on Wages, 2019: Universal applicability with tiered bonus provisions

This code consolidates the Payment of Wages Act, Minimum Wages Act, Payment of Bonus Act, and Equal Remuneration Act into a single framework with simplified compliance.

### Employee count thresholds

| Provision | Threshold | Notes |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Minimum wages | **No threshold** | Applicable to ALL employees from day one |
| Payment of wages | **No threshold** | No wage ceiling; covers all employees |
| Bonus payment | **20+ employees** | On any day during accounting year |
| Register exemption | **≤5 employees** | For agriculture/domestic purposes only |

### Wage definitions and the 50% rule

The code defines "wages" as **basic pay + dearness allowance + retaining allowance**. A critical provision states that if excluded components (HRA, bonus, conveyance, overtime, commission) exceed **50% of total remuneration**, the excess is reclassified as wages. SMEs must restructure compensation to ensure basic wages constitute at least half of total CTC.

**Wage ceiling for bonus calculation**: Currently ₹21,000/month (pending final notification). Bonus ranges from **8.33% minimum to 20% maximum**, payable within 8 months of accounting year close.

### Entity type coverage

| Entity Type | Covered | Specific Provisions |
|-------------|---------|---------------------|
| Private Limited Company | ✅ Yes | Defined under Companies Act, 2013 |
| LLP | ✅ Yes | Included in "company" definition for offences |
| Proprietorship | ✅ Yes | Covered under establishment definition |
| Partnership Firm | ✅ Yes | Per Indian Partnership Act, 1932 |
