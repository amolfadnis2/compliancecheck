How this is calculated

The calculator steps through every month until you retire. Each month, you put in 12% of your PF wage (plus any VPF). Your employer’s 12% is split: 8.33% of wages up to the ceiling goes to your pension (EPS), and the rest goes to your PF.

  • Your salary rises by the hike % in your appraisal month each year. If your employer caps PF at ₹25,000, raises above that don’t increase your PF.
  • Interest is worked out monthly on your running balance but added only on 31 March, as EPFO does. That’s why the effective return comes out slightly under the declared rate.
  • Past years use declared rates (8.25% for FY 2025-26). Future years use the rate you choose. EPFO declares the rate every year, so it isn’t guaranteed.
  • Return on your money is the yearly rate at which only what you put in (opening balance plus your contributions) grows into the final corpus. It’s higher than the PF rate because your employer’s share comes free. In the old regime, it counts your 80C tax saving.
  • Interest on your own contributions above ₹2.5 lakh a year is taxable. We track that portion separately and show the tax at your slab.
  • EPS pension = pensionable salary × years of service ÷ 70, capped at the ceiling for each period, with 2 bonus years if you serve 20+ years to 58. Minimum ₹1,000 a month. The EPS 2026 paragraph text is still being confirmed, so treat this as an estimate.

Questions people ask

Does a salary hike always increase my PF?

Only if your employer pays PF on your full Basic + DA. Many employers cap it at the wage ceiling, now ₹25,000. Then your contribution stays at ₹3,000 a month however much your salary rises.

What is the EPF interest rate for 2025-26?

8.25%. The Central Board of Trustees recommended it on 2 March 2026, and EPFO issued crediting instructions on 1 July 2026.

What changed on 17 September 2026?

The EPF wage ceiling rose from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 a month. Mandatory contributions for anyone earning up to ₹25,000 now apply to their full wage, and the maximum mandatory employee PF rose from ₹1,800 to ₹3,000 a month.

Is my PF interest taxable?

Interest on your own contributions above ₹2.5 lakh a year (₹5 lakh if your employer doesn’t contribute) is taxable each year. Interest on the rest stays tax-free.

How does the Labour Code 50% rule affect PF?

If allowances that don’t count as wages make up more than half your pay, the excess is added back to your PF wage. Low-Basic salary structures will see PF go up.

Where does the employer’s 8.33% go?

To the Employees’ Pension Scheme. It doesn’t show in your PF balance, but it buys a monthly pension from age 58 once you have 10 years of service.
