## 3. Timely windows to exploit (as of Aug 2026)

These are the four dated hooks worth building calendars around. Everything with a **[HOT]** tag below hangs off one of them.

1. **Labour Codes.** In force since 21 Nov 2025; draft Central Rules pre-published 30–31 Dec 2025; final rules were expected ~1 Apr 2026 and state rules are still rolling out unevenly across 28 states. A live "which states have notified what" tracker is the single highest-leverage evergreen asset available right now.
2. **DPDP.** Consent Manager registration opens **13 Nov 2026**; substantive obligations bite **13 May 2027**, no grace period, penalties to ₹250 crore. Two hard countdowns, and DPDP is the highest-priced live assessment (₹2,499). This is the best revenue-per-visitor cluster on the site.
3. **FSSAI.** Amendment regulations effective 11 Mar 2026; from 1 Apr 2026 licences/registrations are **perpetual** (no expiry), registration threshold raised to ₹1.5 cr, state licence to ₹50 cr, inspections moved to risk-based. Huge volume of stale "FSSAI renewal" content on the web to displace.
4. **POSH.** Supreme Court has pushed district-wise compliance surveys and mandatory SHe-Box registration of ICCs; board-report disclosure and licence-renewal refusal are now live enforcement levers. Fear-driven, high-intent.

Also worth noting for accuracy in payroll posts: EPF Scheme 2026 took effect 29 June 2026 (₹15,000 ceiling unchanged, 12%/12% unchanged, heavier electronic filing and exempted-establishment governance); ESIC ceiling stays ₹21,000 (₹25,000 for employees with disability) with coverage extended to IT/BPO/commercial offices in notified states.

## 6. How to prioritise anything not on that list

Score each idea 1–5 on four axes and write the highest total first:

1. **Search demand** — is anyone actually querying this?
2. **Conversion fit** — how short is the path from this post to a paid assessment? A DPDP post beats a GST post even at lower volume, because DPDP is ₹2,499 and live.
3. **Competition gap** — can we plausibly outrank what's there? Fire NOC, LWF and auto-dealer topics are wide open; "GST registration" is a bloodbath.
4. **Decay risk** — how fast does it go stale, and will we actually maintain it? Don't publish 28 state pages you won't update.

## 7. Post template (every post, no exceptions)

1. H1 containing the primary query.
2. One-line italic subtitle framing the question.
3. **"Key facts at a glance"** bullet block — atomic, self-contained, quotable facts. This is what LLMs lift.
4. Body with question-shaped H2s.
5. At least one comparison/threshold **table**.
6. **FAQ section** (5–8 questions) → drives `FAQPage` schema.
7. **Sources** section citing primary government sites (epfindia.gov.in, fssai.gov.in, labour.gov.in, meity.gov.in, irdai.gov.in).
8. Single mapped CTA + 2–4 internal links to sibling posts.
9. Frontmatter: `title`, `description` (<160 chars), `date`, `lastReviewed`, `author`, `tags`, `relatedAssessment`, `draft`.

Match the structure of `content/blog/pf-esi-applicability-employer-guide.mdx` — it is the reference implementation.

## 8. Internal linking rules

- Every post links **up** to its cluster pillar and **sideways** to 2–3 siblings.
- Every assessment landing page links **down** into its 3 strongest cluster posts.
