## Wage Thresholds

| Amount | Provision | Impact | Source |
|--------|-----------|--------|--------|
| **Rs.15,000/month** | EPF mandatory ceiling | Above can opt out | EPF Scheme Para 26A |
| **Rs.18,000/month** | Supervisor exclusion | Not "worker" under Codes | Code on Wages S.2(z) |
| **Rs.21,000/month** | ESI coverage ceiling | No ESI above | ESI Act S.2(9) |
| **Rs.21,000/month** | Bonus eligibility | No bonus above | Bonus Act S.2(13) |
| **Rs.25,000/month** | ESI (PwD) ceiling | Higher for disabled | ESI Notification 2017 |
| **Rs.7,000/month** | Bonus calculation ceiling | Max bonus calc base | Bonus Act S.12 |

## Filing Deadlines

| Compliance | Due Date | Penalty for Delay |
|------------|----------|-------------------|
| EPF Monthly | 15th of following month | 12% p.a. + Rs.5/day |
| ESI Monthly | 15th of following month | 12% p.a. + damages |
| Professional Tax | State-specific (usually monthly) | Interest + penalty |
| GST Returns | Monthly/Quarterly | Rs.50/day (max Rs.10,000) |
| Form 16 | June 15 | Rs.100/day (max Rs.5,000) |
| Annual Returns | November 1 | Rs.50,000 |

## Validation Checklist

When validating thresholds in questions:

- [ ] Employee count matches official threshold
- [ ] Wage ceiling reflects latest notification
- [ ] Penalty amount is from current Act/Rules
- [ ] Deadline matches filing calendar
- [ ] Source section/rule is cited correctly
- [ ] Industry exceptions are noted
- [ ] State variations are acknowledged
