| Equal remuneration violation | Rs.50,000 | Rs.1,00,000 | S.54 |

## GST Penalties

| Offence | Penalty |
|---------|---------|
| Late filing of return | Rs.50/day (max Rs.10,000) |
| Non-registration | Tax due + 100% penalty |
| Wrong availment of ITC | 100% of ITC wrongly availed |
| Invoice without supply | 100% of tax amount |
| E-way bill violation | Rs.10,000 or tax evaded (higher) |

## Companies Act Penalties

| Offence | Penalty | Who is liable |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| Non-filing of annual return | Rs.50,000 to Rs.5,00,000 | Company + Directors |
| Non-appointment of auditor | Rs.25,000/day (max Rs.5,00,000) | Company |
| Non-holding of AGM | Rs.1,00,000 (Co), Rs.25,000 (Officer) | Both |
| CSR non-compliance | 2x unspent amount | Company + Directors |

## Compounding Provisions

| Act | Compoundable? | Max Amount |
|-----|---------------|------------|
| EPF Act | Yes (before prosecution) | As notified by CPFC |
| ESI Act | Yes | 50% of damages |
| Factories Act | Some offences | State-specific |
| GST | Yes (non-fraud) | 150% of tax |

## Validation Checklist

When validating penalty information:

- [ ] Penalty amount is from current Act (not pre-amendment)
- [ ] First vs repeat offence distinction noted
- [ ] Imprisonment term included where applicable
- [ ] Responsible persons identified correctly
- [ ] Compounding option mentioned if available
- [ ] Section number is accurate
- [ ] Penalty for fatal accidents included (OSH)
